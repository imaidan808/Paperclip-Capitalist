# js/audio: the sound layer

Everything is generated in the browser with the Web Audio API. There are no audio files, so the site stays a static folder with no build step. The contract is `docs/AUDIO.md`;
this file says how it is built and where the engine chose differently from the document.

| File | What |
|---|---|
| `palette.js` | the key (D major pentatonic), the shared noise (built in the frequency domain, one realisation at 48 kHz for every device), the layer renderer (`voice`), the instruments (marimba, glass, bell, coin, wood, thump, paper, swoosh, stamp, brass, horn, click, sparkle, the mid `body` of a refusal), `moneySize`, the master chain and its soft clip |
| `recipes.js` | the 90 ids and 188 variants as data (`RECIPES`), with class, player flag, rank and cooldown tables; trims come from `trims.js` |
| `trims.js` | calibrated trims in dB and the places in the noise of the sounds that need them (`noiseAt`), written by `node scripts/audio-check.mjs --calibrate --pools` (the arithmetic is `scripts/audio-calib.mjs`, tested in `tests/audio-calibration.test.mjs`); never edited by hand |
| `ambient.js` | the 16 beds, 4 modifiers, 18 one-shots, and the controller (crossfade, night/rain/wealth, one timer chain, the rate rule) |
| `resolve.js` | the collector's resolver (two sentences per burst, the reward budget, merges), the clock gates, state sounds, the hold, catch-up. Pure |
| `engine.js` | `createAudio()`: the context, buses and chain, voices and stealing, rank-aware cooldowns, token buckets, ducking, the ambient bed, the dial tone, hidden-tab and idle handling |
| `settings.js` | `settings.audio`: defaults, migration, presets, the one mute switch (`toggleMute`, `speakerState`), the Sound section of the settings dialog, `whyDropped` |
| `wire.js` | `initAudio()` (page-wide), `wireGame(ctx)` (per game), `wireAudio(ctx)` (both), and the pure tables `eventCalls`, `noteCalls`, `uiCalls`, `dealCalls`, `controlFor` |
| `dialtone.js` | the repair dial tone as the Deal Room says it (the Dial tone switch, the line under the dial, the one announcement per run): `dialInfo`, `dialEarText`, `dialAnnounceText`, `dialSwitchHTML`, `toggleDial`, `tellOnce`. Pure |
| `audition.js` | what the audition page tells its listener: `explainDrop` (every reason the engine can count, `budget` told apart by state), `ambientWhy`, `oneShotWhy`, `dialWhy`, `pressBurst`, `describeBurst`, `describeMinute`. No DOM; the engine is a parameter |
| `instance.js`, `index.js` | the one engine of the page; the public face and `legacySfx` |

Checks: `node --test tests/audio.test.mjs` (no browser; a recording fake context) and `npm run audio` (renders every sound offline in headless Chrome and holds the levels, peaks, DC, clipping,
lengths, brightness, the mid body, the ladders, the beds and the tier gaps through the master chain). `/dev/sounds.html` is the page to listen on (grouped by meaning, with gain/loss pairs, ladders, every pool member, the dial tone and a minute of play through the real wiring). `node scripts/audio-lessons.mjs` mounts the ten Academy lessons with the sound layer wired and fails if one does not start.

## Where this differs from, or settles, the document

- Settings live in `settings.audio` (7.1). The flat keys `sound`, `vol`, `sfxUi`, `sfxGame` and `ambient` mirror it (`mirrorFlat`), and an old save with only those is migrated.
- `moneySize` uses the table's thresholds ($41, $1,780, $75,000), not the log formula in the prototype (they differ by under 1%; the table is what the tests quote).
- The legacy `sfx.*` names become no-ops only while a game is wired (`wireGame`), not as soon as `initAudio()` has run, so the title and the draft keep their sounds until the shell calls `wireGame`.
- Pool members are calibrated one by one (`id#variant@k` in `trims.js`): the pitch of a pool member changes how loud it reads.
- The body of the refusal and loss family uses a sine that steps down to A4 (D5 to A4 for a failure, A4 to F#4 for a gentle "no"; D4 at 294 Hz is 7 dB under 1 kHz for a quiet laptop and was the reason the family sat under its tier) and a knock at 440 Hz, so the fall of a loss is in the band a laptop plays; every variant of the family measures at least 40% above 250 Hz.
- The volume is a power of 1.5 (not a square) times a headroom of 4.6 dB that sits ahead of the glue compressor and the soft clip, so the default (70) is unity and 100 is 4.6 dB above it (`volumeGain`, `HEADROOM`, `masterGain` in `settings.js`; docs/AUDIO.md 3.2). The Classroom rule has no headroom.
- The calibration balances every sound between two models of a listener, the K-weighted one and a quiet laptop (a 220 Hz high-pass, then A-weighting), and holds the tiers apart under both (`node scripts/audio-check.mjs`, docs/AUDIO.md Appendix F).
- The oscillator budget (160) counts what is alive at the same instant, each layer from its voice's real start (`layerSpans`, `peakAlive`), and a loss is counted (`dropped.osc`). The members of a mix share the cause's pan.
- A preset leaves the volume alone; the speaker button, key M and the Deal Room's switch are one rule (`toggleMute`), and "pressed" means something can be heard (`speakerState`).
- The queued moment is played by scheduling it after the current one (never later than 500 ms), not by a timer.
- Wired by `v2/audio-wire` (the shell's side): `shell.js` calls `wireAudio(ctx)` once per mounted game and takes it down in `destroy`; it emits the `ui` events `close-drawer`, `zone` and `lead`
  (`js/ui/audiocues.js`: a baseline first, so a new district or a returning tab rings nothing), reports the Progress rewards as requests, and has the speaker button's act. `js/ui/worldsound.js`
  turns the World's hover, activation, footsteps and camera into `ui` events and plays (`rectOf` gives the stereo pan; `night` the ambient). The HUD has the speaker button and the Draft plays
  `draft.pick`, `ui.refuse` and `draft.begin` itself. The control table in `wire.js` gained the Deal Room's moves, the title's presses, the instructor's ceremonies, the sounds a control
  has of its own (`OWN`) or always gets from its outcome (`QUIET`), the crate box, the disclosure sound and `data-close`; the toast fallback waits 40 ms so the collector's sound is heard first.
- Wired by `v2/audio-deal`: the Deal Room (`js/ui/deal.js`) chooses every move's sound from what the screen shows, says the cause sounds over the page's event bus as `{type:'ui', ui:'deal', what}` (`dealCalls`),
  has its own Sound switch, and marks the buttons that play their own sound `data-snd` (the control table's `dealControl` rows are therefore the fallback for an unmarked deal button, not a second voice); the
  Academy plays `academySounds` (`js/ui/academy-sandbox.js`) at the moments of a lesson.
- One path per trigger, settled at the merge. The room opens with its own `deal.open` (it knows the kind and plays none for a Quick deal), so the dialog observer says nothing for `#dlg.deal`. A buyer clicked with no crate
  ticked is a tick and the toast's nudge: the stock drawer it opens is quiet. A few triggers still make two calls for one sound on purpose, and the engine's same-id cooldown (rank-aware, 120 to 150 ms) admits the first:
  the dock and the drawer's close button (the click, then the shell's `open-drawer`/`close-drawer` event), and a dialog opened or closed by a press (the click, then the dialog observer). Counted in headless Chrome
  against `audio.stats().played`: each of those admits exactly one voice.
- Still not wired (fourth round, docs/AUDIO.md 3.6): the "off screen" case of distance (`opts.where: 'offscreen'` is in the engine and nothing sets it) and the buyer's own place for a sale's pan (the `pitch` event carries only the district); "elsewhere" is wired for a manager's cycle, freight arriving and customs (`awayFrom`, `wire.js`).
- Still not wired: the engine's `kind`/`reason`/`delay`/`pid` fields (the wiring matches the note text and treats a pool sale as immediate until they land); `repair.stop` by precision needs the room's own answer.
- Third round, track X (docs/AUDIO.md Appendix F): `pendingRoom(room)` in `wire.js` applies the Classroom rule from the Draft on (the Draft is before `S.joinRoom`, and `draft.begin` is loud); the Deal Room's repair switch, line and announcement live in `dialtone.js`; `announce()` (`js/ui/common.js`) also writes inside an open modal dialog, because a live region outside it is not in the accessibility tree. `node --test tests/audio-r2x.test.mjs tests/audio-r2x-page.test.mjs` and `node scripts/audio-r2x-check.mjs` (headless Chrome) hold them.
- Fourth round (docs/AUDIO.md Appendix G): the Academy's sandbox no longer undoes the room rule (`roomCheck`), the repair tone follows `audio.dial.active` (`retone()` in `js/ui/deal.js`), the first three door bells of a run bypass the id's cooldown, a press that closes the dialog is one voice (`PRESS_CLOSES_MS`; `release(keepMs)`), a student on the way into a room gets one note about sound, equal-power crossfades between beds (`equalFade`, `AMB.CROSS`), the ceilings hold the 95th percentile of 32 engine-like plays, the quiet laptop's momentary loudness is a third calibration model, `CLASSROOM_DB` is -6 interface and -8 game, and `jitterOf` gives the loud tier 0.4 of the pitch jitter. `/dev/sounds.html` has a Blind round, Mono, Laptop speaker and a contents list. `node scripts/audio-check.mjs --calibrate --pools` writes the trims and checks (about three minutes); `node scripts/audio-r2x-check.mjs` is the browser half (97 checks).
- Fifth round (docs/AUDIO.md Appendix H): `leaveRoom` (`settings.js`) gives back what the room changed and keeps what the player did, with one note (`leftRoomText`); the engine's Classroom gate reads the variant a request will play (`peekVariant`: by `variant`, else `size`), so the test button's coins are heard in a room; key M and the speaker button raise a toast (`toggleMute`'s `shown`), and inside an open sheet say it there; `copyText` (`js/ui/instructor.js`) copies by the older command where there is no async clipboard, and Copy student link no longer plays `ui.ok` at the click; the Deal Room's repair explanation waits for a dial (`dialsDrawn`, `run.view()`), and its header is sticky from 761 px. `node --test tests/audio-r5.test.mjs` and `node scripts/audio-r5-check.mjs` (headless Chrome) hold them.
