## Open, in the order the judges would fix them

**1. The bank still reads as stickers on the water and the cliffs** (all three judges; `sea-open`, `sea-zoom-1`, `bridge-or-ferry`, `coast-close`). Shadows are still faint: one convincing patch in the strait, a faint smear 90 px under the lone lagoon cloud. Widening `castsShadow` to the rim lobes was tried and breaks `world-cloudform.test.mjs` ("the touched share of the bank's reach is well under all of it": the chunk-baking budget), so it needs the budget argument to be reopened, or a different shape. Judges' fixes: a blurred copy of each cluster's silhouette offset down-right and multiplied (about #1c6f8f at 0.2 over water, mauve at 0.25 over land); outline only the outer silhouette (the hairline shows inside the mass wherever lobes overlap); a bank-level underside gradient and ambient occlusion; cap a lobe at about 1.8 times the median (one tower is capped by a sphere four times the others).

**2. Straight banks with no shore treatment** (coast judge, major in `bridge-or-ferry`, minor in `sea-open`, `sea-zoom-2`, `coast-close`). The east bank of the strait is a ruler-straight diagonal about 400 px long, a muddy olive band meeting the water with no sand, no foam, no wet rim; the lagoon's south-west coast and `sea-zoom-2`'s left coast are straight with a thin foam line and no sand. Judges' fix: the same shore stack as the meadows (a 4 to 6 px wet-sand rim, 3 to 4 px scalloped foam with 8 to 14 px wobble, a mint shallow ramp, a few low bumps), with the fog tint applied to the whole stack and the olive pulled toward the warm palette. Not investigated in code: `rasterbase.js` gives sand only to ground under 5.2 high, and the foam film on higher ground is faint (`landShade`, `fm`); the coast's wobble is clamped to a quarter tile (`coastgeo.js`).

**3. The hours are filters, not light** (island and district judges). 17:42: the top-left sea turns khaki (188,189,147 against 83,203,210 by day) and teal props go sage; shadows never move with the hour; 19:00 and 23:00 are blue multiply tints (night mean luminance 121 against 194, sea still saturated cobalt, clouds mid-grey "storm pompoms"), no moon rim, no warm window pools. Fixes named: a hue-preserving soft-light grade that keeps the sea blue; clock-driven shadow length and direction; a darker, desaturated night sea (about #0f1d44) with a cool moon rim on the clouds and warm emissive pools on the open district. Note that `docs/ART_DIRECTION.md` and `tests/world-grade.test.mjs` pin a night that is "never murky" (`GRADE.night`, luma 0.61): a darker night is a change of the author's rule.

**4. Bugs at working zoom.** (a) `fog-near`: a ruler-straight pale translucent panel runs 800 px down the left (x about 0.25) across grass, cliff and shore, and a slanted one over the sea at the lower right. It is in the first render, before this pass. Bisected with `PC_QUERY='&layers=X:legacy' node scripts/world-shots.mjs ... --set fog-near` (a new knob of `scripts/boot.mjs`): it stays with the `clouds`, `terrain` and `blocks` layers each on their legacy drawer, and under `--motion reduced` (so not the light shafts); it is gone with `all:legacy`. The terrain veil (`terrainfield.js`, the nearest locked district) was suspected and ruled out: the veil weight is identical under a continuous rule in this world. Not found. (b) `city-close`: the `$51k` buyer tag covers the "+ Hire" call to action and `$14k` covers the BUYERS sign: z-sort and offset the tags. (c) `bridge-or-ferry`: the ferry is a pale lilac ghost (no hull foam, no contact shadow, no ripple) and its NOT RUNNING text overflows its plaque. (d) lamp poles stand behind buyers and fuse with them (`start-close`, `start-mid`).

**5. Nothing lives on the sea, and the piers are flat ribbons** (all three). Judges: iso-aligned rows of crisp swell bands that scale with depth, one or two moving things (a sailboat, a gull's shadow, a fish ring), piers with plank joints, pilings, a shadow and ripple rings; a darker contact shadow at every cliff foot.

> You are an independent, adversarial art judge for a browser game's isometric world (a business simulation for university entrepreneurship classes; an island of 16 districts, the locked ones hidden under cloud banks by design; the look is a bright, cozy TOY DIORAMA, Untitled Goose Game and Egg Inc., not a flat vector ad). Judge pictures only; change nothing. Your area: AREA. The AFTER pictures are PATHS (2880x1800, a 1440x900 window at device scale 2); the BEFORE picture for the "change" score is PATH. Score the AFTER build 0 to 10 on three lenses (8 means "ship it", 5 "visibly unfinished"): change (how unmistakable the improvement at normal viewing size), quality (toy-diorama charm, one consistent warm light, silhouettes, depth, life, readability as a game), edges (seams, stair-steps, halos, banding, clipped sprites, z-order, text collisions, anything that looks like a bug). Be specific: every issue must be something you SAW, with severity (blocker, major: a casual player sees it, minor), a region as fractions of the picture, what you see and a concrete fix; at most 15, most severe first. Crop at 1:1 with `sips -c H W --cropOffset Y X IN.png --out OUT.png` and enlarge with `sips -Z 1080` (write crops under a folder you make; at most 8 crops). Finish with ONE JSON object: area, scores {change, quality, edges}, perShot, issues [{shot, severity, region, problem, fix}], verdict (what keeps it from 8 and the three changes that would move it most).

Three agents with this brief cost about 170,000 tokens each and ten minutes.
