## What the cloud session did, job by job

The session was cut short by its credit limit: jobs 1 and 2 are done, job 3 is partly done, job 4 is a first round of finders with a handful of repairs. Every number below was measured in headless Chrome on a 4-core Linux machine; nothing was run on real hardware, Safari, Firefox or with a screen reader.

### 1. The Academy coach card: done, by rule (`d00f6b6`, `af6db6f`)
- **The rule** (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, "The coach card covers nothing"): a reserved strip at the bottom of a small window for the card or its tab (`html.ac-strip`), the card placed only on exact free spots between the controls (`freeSpots`), smaller (slim, narrow) before it folds, a card whose words would scroll is its labelled tab (never cut), the words one tap or the key C away, the fold announced; a fold the player chose stays (`held`). The card the player opens is the full card, or the same words across the strip's width where the full card is taller than the window (`wide`, `OPEN_SIZES`).
- **The sweep** (`scripts/academy-sweep.mjs`, page half `academy-sweep-page.js`): every lesson step x 6 window sizes x mouse/touch x every state (panels, Deal Room stages, hire dialog, Cash, menu, alert) x variants (arrival, hint due, toasts, largest text, "Skip this lesson?", closed early). `--level quick` measured 9,840 cells at 768x1024, 844x390, 640x360 and 360x640 with no fault except 5 `sweep-error`s (a 180 s cell timeout on a loaded machine; `--cell-ms` now exists). `--level std` found one class, at 844x390: at the largest text the card the player opens had 42% to 89% of its hint behind its own scroll; the `wide` shape fixed it (lessons 1 to 4 at 844x390 and 640x360 are clean). **The std sweep was stopped before it finished and was not re-run after the fix**: run `node scripts/academy-sweep.mjs --level std --sizes 844x390,640x360,360x640,768x1024 --jobs 2` (about an hour on a quiet machine) and then `--level full`; 768x1024 (both modes) was clean and 360x640 clean for the five lessons it reached.
- Two faults the sweep found *behind* the card were repaired as classes: the Deal Room result's sticky Continue row cut off in a 310 px dialog (`revealFoot`), and the dock drawer's scroll area widened by a screen-reader-only span so focus scrolled its title bar away (`overflow: clip`, `tests/drawer-clip.test.mjs`). `tests/rep-card.test.mjs` lesson 10 is no longer `todo`.

### 4. The whole-product audit: one round of finders, partly repaired
Finders (read-only, each reproduced what it reports) finished for the engine (static), the Deal Room (static) and the relay and static server (both angles); the finders for session and classroom, content, privacy, docs, balance, UI at sizes, accessibility and performance were stopped before they reported. **No skeptic pass was run**: treat the lists below as "reproduced by its author", not as confirmed. Repaired with a test that fails without the fix (`tests/cloud-audit.test.mjs`): relay binary frames cost rate (`rs-binary-bypass`); relay SIGTERM closes clients with 1001 and exits 0 (`rs-sigterm`, `relay-4`); the static server does not follow a symlink out of its tree (`rs-symlink`, `relay-6`); an offer is accepted once (`engine-core-accept-replay`); a throwing event listener does not stop a day (`engine-core-listener-throw-corrupts-day`); `$-12.50` and `1 days` in engine messages; a double-click or held key is one Deal Room move (`deal-room-and-games-double-activation`).

**Open, reproduced by the finder, not repaired** (ids are the finders'; `*` = the finder says it needs the author's decision):
- Relay: `rs-seat-claim` the owner token can never bind once a no-token instructor connection holds the seat; `rs-keytable-lockout` / `relay-1` / `relay-2` idle or snapshotting sockets fill a room's pid table or the relay's slots, and a classmate who has seen a Founder ID can pre-bind it; `relay-3`* no Origin check; `relay-5`* chat and presence share the 32 KB cap (amplification); `rs-csp-missing` / `relay-8`* no Content-Security-Policy and no `nosniff`; `rs-pages-unpinned` / `relay-7`* the Pages workflow's actions are pinned to major tags and its test job holds `pages:write` / `id-token:write`.
- Deal Room and engine: `deal-room-and-games-read-xp-oracle` (blocker as filed) the XP on a walk-away card is computed from the passed lot's hidden truth, so a Read tells you the truth for free; `deal-room-and-games-savefile-truth`* the downloaded save has every lot's hidden truth; `engine-core-first-sale-dead-end`* selling the first paperclip for less than the cheapest lot strands a founder; `engine-core-hash01-clustered-events` `hash01` has no final mix, so 44% of worlds never get an algorithmic event and 27% get 20 or more (fixing it changes every seeded event: re-run the calibration); `deal-room-and-games-auction-worth-stale`, `-wreck-verdict`, `-pro-assist-hidden`, `-afford-gates`, `-read-prior-miscalibrated` (wrong or contradictory numbers on the Bid, Close and Read cards); `engine-core-fees-unbounded`, `-lastauto-handpitch`*, `-nw-envelope-early`* (the envelope is below honest fast play on days 7 to 12), `-projected-digital-quality`, `-veteran-softens-boom`*, `-rewind-future-target`; `deal-room-and-games-pitch-scout`*, `-rewind-unmarks`*, `-slider-dominates-dial`*, `-assist-free-solves-close`*, `-first-move-unwarned`.
